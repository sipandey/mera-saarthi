Delete Your Mera Saarthi Account

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you may request the deletion of your Mera Saarthi account and associated personal data at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Mera Saarthi app on your Android smartphone.
  2. Tap the ℹ️ (Info) icon in the top header.
  3. Scroll down to the Account Closure & Deletion section.
  4. Tap Delete Account & Anonymize History and confirm.

Your account, active listings, vehicle documents, and personal details will be immediately removed and scheduled for evidence purging.

What Happens to Your Data?

  • Immediate Removal: Your profile, name, phone number, and vehicle listings are immediately deactivated.
  • Document Purging: All uploaded Aadhaar, selfie, RC, and insurance image bytes are permanently deleted from cloud storage.
  • Booking History: Operational booking records are permanently anonymized to retain aggregate trip metrics for taxi stand accounting.
