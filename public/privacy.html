Mera Saarthi Privacy Policy

Welcome to Mera Saarthi ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users, passengers, and driver partners in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to operate our rural taxi coordination service:

  • Account Details: Mobile phone number and optional full name used for authentication.
  • Location Data: Approximate and precise location data when you use the "Use my location" feature to find pickup addresses. We do not track your location in the background or during trips.
  • Driver Verification Documents: Driver selfie, vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), commercial insurance, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificates submitted by vehicle owners for manual safety verification.
  • Booking Information: Pickup location, vehicle selection, ride type (local/outstation), and booking timestamp.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To enable passengers and local taxi operators to connect directly for rides.
  • To verify driver and vehicle compliance before listings are activated.
  • To facilitate direct phone contact between passenger and driver once a ride is accepted.
  • To maintain audit receipts of completed or canceled rides for local stand dispute resolution.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. Data is shared strictly as necessary to execute the ride:

  • When a ride request is confirmed, your phone number and pickup details are shared with the driver (and driver details with you) via system dialer links.
  • We do not share your documents or phone numbers with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Evidence Retention & 30-Day Purge Policy

To respect your privacy while ensuring passenger safety:

  • Verification document images (selfies, RC, insurance copies) are retained in secure, encrypted cloud storage only during verification and are automatically purged within 30 days of review.
  • Operational booking receipts are retained in anonymized form for accounting and dispute resolution.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and associated personal information at any time directly through the app in the Help & Privacy menu, or via our web portal at merasaarthi.in/delete-account.
